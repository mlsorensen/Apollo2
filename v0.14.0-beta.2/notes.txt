
### Changes

- **Back up your settings to the memory card.** Settings > Apollo > **Backup**
  writes every preference — paired machine, scale, theme, chime, target weight,
  flush and brew settings, timezone — to `/Apollo2/settings.txt` on the same
  card your shots are on, and restores it onto another Apollo. Replacing the
  hardware no longer means setting everything up again: move the card across
  and tap **Restore from card**.

  The page says what the inserted card holds (when the backup was taken, and on
  which firmware and board), and a restore replaces every setting and restarts
  to apply it. Anything the backup doesn't carry returns to its default, so an
  older backup lands exactly as an upgrade would. Restoring between different
  boards works and is the point. A backup written by newer firmware than the
  machine you're restoring onto is refused — update it first.

  The file is plain text, so anyone with the card can read it. Your **WiFi
  network** and your **Micra pairing token** are each a switch on the backup
  confirmation: both on by default, because the point is a replacement that
  just works — turn one off and you set that up by hand on the new machine
  instead. Nothing is ever backed up or restored on its own.
