# Stats tab: temperature history with a tap-to-zoom window, device info from the standard DIS, a manual clock

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Area:** ux
- **First decided:** 2026-06-29 · **Last changed:** 2026-09-08
- **Boards:** all; History needs an SD slot

## Context

The third bottom tab is Stats. Heat-up and cool-down are only visible with
a temperature history, and the machine exposes no clock over the local
link, so time has to come from the device itself. The shot log (History)
arrived with the SD card work and is covered by the shot-history records,
not this one.

## Decision

- **Brew / Boiler / History / Info** behind a segmented selector, mirroring
  Settings.
- Brew and Boiler plot the boiler temperature against time from an
  in-memory ring sampled every 30 s while connected (`core::IHistory`;
  `platform::History::kSampleIntervalS`, capacity 24 h plus slack), with
  a set-point reference line, greyed no-data spans (before recording, and
  BLE gaps), and a +/- zoom over the time window. The selector names the
  section and the axis gives the values; nothing sits above the chart
  ([what was dropped](#alternatives-and-dead-ends)).
- Info reads the machine's standard Bluetooth Device Information Service
  (manufacturer, model, serial, firmware, software) once per connect, plus
  an Uptime row and an honest "NTP synced" state ([when added](#history)).
- **Time is manual or NTP, never from the machine**
  ([why](#alternatives-and-dead-ends)). The internal RTC is set from a Set
  time page with a 12/24-hour toggle; WiFi + NTP sets it when configured
  (`2026-07-01-wifi-ntp-and-static-ip.md`). Boards with a battery-backed
  RTC keep time across power-off.

## Consequences

- The temperature ring is RAM only: 24 h at 30 s is ~2,880 samples, no
  database ([alternatives](#alternatives-and-dead-ends)).
- Screenshots: `../img/manual/stats-graphs.png`,
  `../img/manual/stats-history.png`, `../img/manual/stats-info.png`.

### Alternatives and dead ends

- Syncing the clock from the machine over BLE: not possible, no
  characteristic carries a date or time.
- A database for the temperature history: unnecessary, 24 h at 30 s is
  ~2,880 samples in RAM.
- A title, value and zoom button above the chart: dropped for the overlaid
  +/- zoom; the selector names the section and the axis gives the values.
