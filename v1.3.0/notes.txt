
### Features

- **Record your dose, see the ratio.** Put the dosed portafilter on the bench scale and tap Record; the graph then shows dose, yield and brew ratio live, and History keeps all three. A Default dose switch stages a fixed dose for every shot instead.
- **Live readings in the graph legend.** The legend shows the espresso scale's flow rate or weight and the bench scale's grams, not just the units. The readings line up on their decimal point and the units stand in a column, so nothing shifts as the numbers change.
- **Detailed log switch.** Settings > Apollo > Display > Detailed log keeps the minute-by-minute housekeeping in the diagnostic log; off, the log holds events only and lasts weeks.
- **Auto-standby counts inactivity.** A flush or backflush restarts the auto-standby count like a shot does, and nothing fires in the middle of one. The row is now "Auto-standby when idle".
- **Paddle wiring setting.** Settings > Micra > Controls > Paddle wiring pins a P4 board to the Apollo Link or the opto cable, so the unused pins are ignored. Auto, the default, watches both as before.
- **Paddle breaker.** A paddle sense input that flickers on its own now opens the paddle drive, hands the paddle back to the machine on an Apollo Link, and shows a modal with Reset. Ordinary use, backflushing included, does not trip it.
- **Umbra battery estimate.** A paired Umbra's battery reading freezes once it sleeps, so Apollo estimates it from a daily drain rate, shown as `~41%`. Calibrate drain measures your scale's real figure.

### Fixes
- **Settings restore keeps the Paddle wiring choice.** The per-unit Paddle wiring setting (Auto / Apollo Link / Opto cable) is left out of the backup file on purpose, but a restore used to erase it and fall back to Auto. It now survives a restore like the pad repair knobs.

- **Fewer nuisance disconnects with a paired Umbra.** An unanswered Micra request is re-dialled after 5 s with no visible disconnect, instead of a 30 s drop and reconnect. A queued command still lands.
- **Device settings page no longer blank for a scale that has none.** A Varia Aku now shows "No device settings available for this scale" instead of an empty page.
- **Graph no longer comes up empty on the 8" box.** Changing Bench scale on Home could leave the flow graph blank until a restart.
